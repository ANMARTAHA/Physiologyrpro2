Ovarian cycle

  • Follicular phase: dominant follicle selected ~day 6; the rest become atretic (apoptosis).
  • Ovulatory phase: follicle ruptures ~day 14; ovum enters the tube.
  • Luteal phase: corpus luteum secretes estrogen + progesterone; degenerates day ~24 without pregnancy.

Uterine cycle

  • Proliferative (days 5–14): estrogen regrows the endometrium.
  • Secretory (post-ovulation): progesterone + estrogen make glands coiled and secretory.
  • Menstruation: spiral artery spasm (prostaglandins) → necrosis → bleeding.

Two-cell, two-gonadotropin theory

  • Theca interna: LH receptors → cholesterol → androstenedione.
  • Granulosa: FSH receptors → aromatase → androgens become estradiol.
  • Mature granulosa cells gain LH receptors too.

Feedback control

  • Steady, moderate estrogen → negative feedback on LH.
  • High, sustained estrogen (late follicular) → positive feedback → LH surge.
  • Luteal phase: estrogen + progesterone + inhibin keep LH/FSH low.

Fertilization & implantation

  • Occurs in the ampulla of the uterine tube.
  • Acrosome reaction → acrosin helps zona penetration; fertilin fuses membranes.
  • Blastocyst implants ~3 days later, usually on the dorsal uterine wall.

Pregnancy & parturition

  • hCG rescues the corpus luteum; placenta takes over by week 6–8.
  • hCS acts as a "maternal growth hormone," sparing glucose for the fetus.
  • Oxytocin + prostaglandins + cervical stretch = positive-feedback labor loop.

Topics

The full topic map for this unit — use it to check what's covered and jump into any tab for detail.

1 · Reproduction — basics

  • Gametes: sperm & ovum
  • Gonads: testes & ovaries
  • Zygote → embryo → fetus → birth

2 · Sex determination

  • Genotype & sex chromosomes (X vs Y)
  • XY = male, XX = female — fixed at fertilization
  • Gametogenesis: spermatogenesis & oogenesis, meiosis I/II, polar bodies

3 · Sex differentiation

  • Gonadal differentiation: urogenital ridge, SRY gene (week 7)
  • Internal ducts: Wolffian vs Müllerian, role of AMH & testosterone
  • External genitalia: role of DHT — penis/scrotum vs female genitalia

4 · Gonadal hormones

  • Androgens (masculinizing) vs estrogens (feminizing) — both sexes make both
  • Adrenal cortex as a second androgen source; peripheral aromatization
  • Inhibin B, FSH/LH, and the feedback loop (cyclic in females, noncyclic in males)

5 · Puberty & adrenarche

  • Adolescence vs puberty (strict definition)
  • Girls: thelarche → pubarche → menarche
  • Adrenarche & 17α-hydroxylase; fetal/neonatal testosterone bursts

6 · Menopause

  • Perimenopause vs menopause; average onset age
  • Follicle depletion → ↓estrogen/progesterone/inhibin → ↑FSH before LH
  • Symptoms: hot flushes, night sweats; disease risk (osteoporosis, IHD)

7 · Ovarian & uterine cycle

  • Follicular → ovulatory → luteal phases
  • Proliferative → secretory endometrium; menstruation mechanism
  • Two-cell, two-gonadotropin theory (theca/LH, granulosa/FSH)

8 · Fertilization & pregnancy

  • Fertilization in the ampulla; acrosome reaction; block to polyspermy
  • Implantation (~3 days later); hCG rescues the corpus luteum
  • hCS as the "maternal growth hormone" of pregnancy

9 · Parturition

  • Average pregnancy length: 270 days from fertilization
  • Estrogen, fetal CRH/cortisol, oxytocin-receptor rise
  • Positive-feedback labor loop: stretch → oxytocin → contractions

The SRY → AMH → testosterone → DHT relay

  • SRY (Y chromosome): triggers testis formation — nothing else.
  • AMH (Sertoli cells): Müllerian ducts regress.
  • Testosterone (Leydig cells): Wolffian ducts persist → epididymis, vas deferens, seminal vesicles.
  • DHT (from testosterone in target tissue): external genitalia → penis, scrotum.
  • No functional testis = default female pathway, regardless of genotype.

Don't mix these up

  • Thelarche = breast development (first sign, girls)
  • Pubarche = axillary/pubic hair
  • Menarche = first period (ovulation regular ~1 yr later)
  • Adrenarche = rise in adrenal androgens, separate axis from gonadal puberty

Menopause hormone pattern

  • ↓ Estradiol & progesterone (ovary stops secreting)
  • ↑ FSH before LH — inhibin B loss selectively releases FSH
  • Small residual estrogen: peripheral aromatization of adrenal androstenedione
  • Root cause = follicle depletion, not a pituitary problem

Gonadal secretion patterns

  • Ovary: mostly estrogen, some androgen
  • Testis: mostly androgen, some estrogen
  • Both sexes: adrenal cortex adds androgens too
  • Inhibin B → selectively suppresses FSH

Chromosomes & meiosis quick numbers

  • Primary spermatocyte / oocyte: 46 chromosomes
  • After meiosis I (secondary spermatocyte/oocyte, 1st polar body): 23
  • Mature gamete: 23 → zygote after fertilization: 46
  • Oogenesis only completes meiosis II at fertilization

Cycle & pregnancy key numbers

  • Cycle: 28 d average, ovulation day 14, luteal phase fixed at 14 d
  • Menstrual blood loss: ~30 mL average, >80 mL abnormal
  • Pregnancy: 270 days from fertilization
  • hCG detectable: blood ~6 days, urine ~14 days post-conception
